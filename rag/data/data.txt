# Copperleaf Restaurant — External Facts

Copperleaf is a modern restaurant focused on seasonal food, fresh ingredients, and sustainable sourcing.

## Food & Menu

* The menu changes periodically to reflect seasonal ingredients and availability.
* Copperleaf uses fresh ingredients from selected local and regional suppliers whenever possible.
* The restaurant offers vegetarian and vegan options.
* Gluten-free and other dietary requirements can often be accommodated when requested in advance.
* Customers with food allergies should inform restaurant staff before ordering.
* Allergen information for menu items is available upon request.
* Menu availability may change depending on ingredient supply and seasonality.
* The restaurant aims to provide balanced menu choices, including lighter and plant-based options.

## Reservations & Dining

* Reservations are recommended during busy periods.
* Friday and Saturday evenings are typically Copperleaf's busiest periods.
* Peak-hour reservations may require advance planning.
* Walk-in availability can vary depending on the day and current capacity.
* Customers should contact the restaurant in advance for large groups or special dining arrangements.

## Takeaway & Orders

* Copperleaf supports takeaway orders.
* Takeaway availability may depend on the menu item and current restaurant capacity.
* Customers requesting takeaway should confirm estimated preparation and collection times.
* Special dietary requirements should be communicated when placing takeaway orders.

## Sustainability

* Copperleaf prioritizes suppliers that follow responsible sourcing practices.
* The restaurant considers food-safety standards when selecting suppliers.
* Seasonal sourcing helps reduce reliance on ingredients that are unavailable locally during certain periods.
* Copperleaf aims to reduce unnecessary food waste through responsible purchasing and preparation practices.
* The restaurant supports environmentally conscious food-sourcing practices where practical.

## Customer Service

* Restaurant staff can provide information about ingredients and common allergens upon request.
* Customers should communicate dietary restrictions before ordering so staff can determine suitable options.
* Special requests may require advance notice.
* Staff can help customers identify vegetarian, vegan, and other suitable menu choices.

## Supplier & Food Safety

* Copperleaf works with selected food suppliers rather than sourcing ingredients indiscriminately.
* Supplier selection takes responsible sourcing and food-safety practices into consideration.
* Fresh ingredients are handled according to appropriate food-safety procedures.
* Ingredient availability may vary based on supplier stock and seasonal conditions.
